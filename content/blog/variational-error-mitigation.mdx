Variational quantum algorithms are notoriously sensitive to coherent and stochastic noise. Error mitigation layers can recover
useful expectation values without full-blown error correction.

## Composite mitigation stack

1. **Measurement error mitigation** using tensor-product calibration matrices.
2. **Probabilistic error cancellation** with Pauli twirling.
3. **Zero-noise extrapolation** with gate stretching on hardware-efficient ansätze.

## Benchmark setup

We evaluate each technique on a 16-qubit heavy-hex device with a VQE ansatz targeting the LiH ground state. Cross entropy is
used as a stability metric across circuit depths.

| Technique | Energy Error (mHa) | Shot Budget |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| Baseline (no mitigation) | 23.1 | 5k |
| Measurement mitigation | 12.4 | 5k |
| PEC + ZNE | 5.9 | 20k |

The resource overhead is considerable, but hybrid mitigation pipelines maintain chemical accuracy within realistic shot
budgets.
