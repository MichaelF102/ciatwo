Quantum support vector machines encode classical data via feature maps $\phi(x)$ implemented by quantum circuits. The kernel is
evaluated as $k(x, x') = |\langle 0 | U_\phi(x)^{\dagger} U_\phi(x') | 0 \rangle|^2$ and fed into a classical SVM optimizer. The
power of the approach hinges on the expressivity of $U_\phi$ and the ability to estimate overlaps with low variance.
